FlowChatInter-Bold.ttf is the Latin subset of Inter 3.012 (git-06b166889)
served by Twitch, instantiated at weight 700 and converted to TrueType.
Only its family/style names were changed to FlowChatInter; glyph metrics
and outlines come from the original weight instance.

Source: https://assets.twitch.tv/assets/inter-latin-variable-wghtOnly-normal-a05a0d687a088c4efed3.woff2

Converted with FontTools `varLib.instancer.instantiateVariableFont`,
using `{"wght": 700}`, and saved with `flavor = None`.
The SIL Open Font License is in OFL.txt and embedded in the font's name table.
